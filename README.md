Mechancis Only of Frank Stone Battery Escape
 
Start​

⬇️​

Explore the room (Use flashlight to navigate dark areas)​

⬇️​

Find batteries?​

➡️ Torch Battery​
→ Press Right-click to collect​
→ Press Q to open inventory​
→ Use with Z to recharge the torch​
→ Press Q again to close inventory​

➡️ Door Battery​
→ Press Right-click to collect​
→ Press Q to open inventory​
→ Use E to place in door generator​
→ Press Q to close inventory​

⬇️​

Torch battery low?​

➡️ Yes → Open inventory (Q) → Use battery (Z) → Close inventory (Q)​
➡️ No → Keep exploring​

⬇️​

Enemy encounter?​
➡️ Torch BATTERY >0 → Scares enemy away​
➡️ Torch BATTERY =0→ Enemy chases player​

⬇️​

All 4 door batteries placed?​
➡️ Yes → Green icon appears​

⬇️​

Interact with lever (E)​

⬇️​

Door opens → ESCAPE!​



-------------------------------------------------------


Key Features:​

Torch batteries recharge flashlight; required for exploration​

Four door batteries must be found and inserted to progress​

Flashlight battery drains over time and needs managing​

Roaming AI enemy chases the player; can be scared away with light​

Escape is only possible after activating a lever, unlocked when all door batteries are collected​

Full inventory system with item inspection, usage, and stacking logic​

Visual feedback for item use, power levels, and door status​
